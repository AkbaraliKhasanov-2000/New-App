Scanlet turns your iPhone into a fast, private PDF scanner. Scan documents, receipts, IDs, contracts and handwritten notes, recognize text with on-device OCR, and share clean, searchable PDFs in seconds.

SCAN LIKE A PRO
• Automatic edge detection, perspective correction and multi-page capture
• Smart filters: Auto, Vivid, Grayscale and Black & White remove shadows and sharpen text
• Import photos and PDFs from Photos and Files — documents in photos are cropped automatically
• Manual crop with four-corner adjustment, rotate, reorder and delete pages

IMAGE TO TEXT (OCR)
• Recognize printed text in dozens of languages, right on your iPhone
• Copy, share or translate recognized text
• Search inside every scan from the library
• Create searchable PDFs you can select and search in any PDF app

SIGN & PROTECT
• Draw your signature once and place it on any page
• Lock PDFs with a password before sharing
• Protect the whole app with Face ID

ORGANIZE & SHARE
• Folders, favorites, sorting, grid and list views
• Merge several scans into one document
• Export as PDF, JPG or text; choose A4, US Letter or fit-to-image paper size
• Share by Mail, Messages, AirDrop, save to Files or print

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Scanlet has no account and no cloud. Your documents and recognized text stay on your device.

Works with Siri and Shortcuts: say “Scan a document with Scanlet”.
