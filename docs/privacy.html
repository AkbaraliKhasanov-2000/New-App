Privacy Policy

Scanlet: PDF Doc Scanner & OCR · Effective September 29, 2026

Summary

Scanlet does not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK and no advertising.

Your documents

Scanned pages, recognized text, signatures and folders are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's private container. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework; images are never uploaded.

Camera and Face ID

The camera is used only while you scan. Face ID / Touch ID is handled entirely by iOS; Scanlet never receives biometric data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Scanlet only receives a confirmation that your subscription is active; it never sees your payment details.

Sharing

Files leave the app only when you choose to share, export, save or print them.

Children

Scanlet does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Email support@scanlet.app.
