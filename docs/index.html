How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Scanmuse Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked.

Open Scanmuse → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Text wasn't recognized correctly.

Scan in good light and hold the phone parallel to the page. For mixed-language documents, choose the languages in Settings → Recognition Languages.

Where are my documents stored?

Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your documents, so export anything important first.
